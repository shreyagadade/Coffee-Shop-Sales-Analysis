# ☕ Coffee Shop Sales Analysis Dashboard

## 📌 Project Overview
An interactive Power BI dashboard designed to analyze coffee shop sales, revenue trends, product performance, and customer purchasing patterns.

The dashboard helps understand business performance and supports data-driven decision-making.

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies
- Power BI
- Power Query
- DAX
- Microsoft Excel

## 📊 Key Performance Indicators
| KPI | Value |
|---|---:|
| Total Revenue | 698.81K |
| Total Transactions | 149K |
| Total Quantity Sold | 214K |
| Average Order Value | 4.69 |

## 📈 Dashboard Visualizations
- Monthly Revenue Trend
- Top 10 Products by Revenue
- Revenue by Store Location
- Transactions by Hour of Day
- Revenue by Product Category
- Revenue by Day of Week

## 🔍 Key Business Insights
1. Barista Espresso is the highest-revenue product type among the displayed top 10.
2. Hell's Kitchen is the highest-revenue store location.
3. 10 AM is the busiest transaction hour.
4. Monday records the highest revenue among the days of the week.
5. Coffee is the highest-revenue product category.
6. Revenue increases from February through June 2023.

## 🧹 Data Preparation
- Imported sales data into Power BI.
- Cleaned and transformed data using Power Query.
- Checked data quality and corrected column data types.
- Created a Revenue calculated column using quantity × unit price.
- Created DAX measures for total revenue, transactions, quantity, and average order value.
- Created calculated columns for transaction hour and day of the week.

## 🎯 Interactive Features
- Store Location slicer
- Product Category slicer
- Transaction Date slicer

## 💡 Project Outcome
Developed an interactive sales dashboard to monitor KPIs, compare store performance, identify popular products, and understand sales patterns.
